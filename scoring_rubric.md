# Scoring Rubric


## Correctness

5 - Fully correct. No factual errors or logical mistakes. Addresses the prompt completely.
4 - Mostly correct. Minor inaccuracies or omissions that do not affect the main answer.
3 - Partially correct. Mixed accurate and inaccurate elements, or addresses only part of the prompt.
2 - Mostly incorrect. Major factual or logical errors, but contains some valid content.
1 - Largely incorrect or off-topic. Wrong answer or fails to address the question.

## Quality

5 - Excellent. Clear, well-structured, appropriate depth, thoughtful, easy to follow.
4 - Good. Generally clear and well-organized with minor weaknesses.
3 - Adequate. Acceptable but with notable weaknesses (unclear structure, awkward phrasing, etc.).
2 - Poor. Significant weaknesses in clarity, structure, or depth.
1 - Very poor. Confusing, badly structured, shallow, or otherwise hard to use.
